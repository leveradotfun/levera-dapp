Every coin is an EIP-1167 clone of `Launch`:

- **1,000,000,000** tokens minted to the launch contract at creation.
- **800,000,000** sit on a constant-product bonding curve.
- **200,000,000** plus the ETH raise seed a TOKEN/ETH AMM at graduation.

The curve is quoted **in ETH**, not USD, and it custodies ETH. A USD-quoted curve holding ETH is insolvent the moment the oracle moves, because it owes dollars it does not have.

USD is snapshotted at the creation-time oracle for **display only**. The on-chain cap is `targetRaiseEth`, so the goalpost cannot drift with the dollar price. Factory floor: `MIN_RAISE_ETH = 0.1 ether` (below that, `virtualEth` can round to zero and buys divide by zero). Headline default: **6.9 ETH**.

## Virtual reserves

Let $T$ be the target raise in ETH and $C = 8 \times 10^8$ the sellable supply (18 decimals on-chain). Shape $m = 4/3$:

$$
m = \frac{4}{3}, \qquad
V_{t0} = C \cdot m, \qquad
V_{u0} = (m - 1) \cdot T
$$

On-chain `CURVE_SHAPE_M = 1.333\ldots \times 10^{18}` (WAD). For $T = 6.9$ ETH:

$$
V_{t0} \approx 1.0667 \times 10^9, \qquad V_{u0} = 2.3\ \mathrm{ETH}
$$

$$
k = V_{t0} \cdot V_{u0}
$$

Selling the full 800M raises **exactly** $T$, and the last curve tick equals the AMM listing:

$$
P_{\mathrm{list}} = \frac{T}{2 \times 10^8}
$$

That identity is why $m = 4/3$: the virtual token surplus is $C/3$, the virtual ETH is $T/3$, so after 800M is sold the spot is $T / 200\mathrm{M}$.

## Buy

Fee first, then constant product on virtual reserves:

$$
\mathrm{fee} = 0.0125 \cdot \mathrm{ethIn}, \qquad
V_u' = V_u + \mathrm{netEth}, \qquad
V_t' = k / V_u', \qquad
\Delta t = V_t - V_t'
$$

Tokens come from `curveSellable` (real inventory). `realEthRaised` tracks net ETH in.

A **creator buy** is an ordinary curve buy (same 1.25% fee). It **does** move the spot: more ETH in $V_u$ means a higher price for everyone after. Graduation listing is independent of how much the creator bought; leftover curve inventory plus the raise still seed 200M / 6.9 ETH.

Logged to `data/events.csv` as a `LAUNCH` row: `launch_spot_eth`, `price_after_creator_buy_eth`, `creator_buy_impact_pct`, each with a `_wei` companion so a float64 read cannot silently render them as zero.

## Sell

Solve for the gross ETH out of the virtual pool, then take 1.25% off that gross. Real ETH paid to the seller cannot exceed `realEthRaised`.

## Circulating vs totalSupply

`totalSupply()` is always 1B. Market cap and curve progress must use **circulating** and `800M − curveSellable`, not `totalSupply()`. Using `totalSupply()` for either was a priced-in bug and is fixed.

## Why not USD

If the curve quoted dollars and held ETH, an oracle print that moves $P$ would change the dollar value of inventory without changing the dollar liability of the remaining curve. The book would be insolvent or over-reserved depending on the direction. Quoting and custodying the same asset makes the curve's solvency independent of $P$. USD is a display layer.
