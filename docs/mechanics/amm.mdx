`MemePair` is a Uniswap V2-style TOKEN/WETH pair with one fork.

## Pricing function

$$
y_{\mathrm{price}} =
\begin{cases}
\texttt{reserveEth} & \text{1x} \\
\texttt{juniorEth} = J / P & \text{2x (fall back to reserveEth if } J = 0\text{)}
\end{cases}
$$

$$
k = \texttt{reserveToken} \cdot y_{\mathrm{price}}
$$

Spot:

$$
p_{\mathrm{ETH}} = \frac{y_{\mathrm{price}}}{\texttt{reserveToken}}
$$

On a just-paired 2x book, $y_{\mathrm{price}} \approx 2 \times \texttt{reserveEth}$, so spot is $\sim 2\times$ a 1x listing of the same raise. ETH moving 10% moves junior NAV 20%, which moves $y_{\mathrm{price}}$ 20%, which moves spot 20%. Vault ETH **prices** the book without sitting in it.

## Swaps pay from reserveEth only

### ETH in, tokens out

`Launch` wraps net ETH, sends WETH to the pair, then calls `swapEthForTokens`.

$$
k = r_t \cdot y, \qquad y' = y + \mathrm{ethIn}, \qquad r_t' = k / y', \qquad \Delta t = r_t - r_t'
$$

`reserveEth` increases by `ethIn`. On 2x, $y$ is junior ETH, so the same ETH in buys fewer tokens than on 1x — the levered book is thicker in the pricing coordinate.

### Tokens in, ETH out

Tokens are transferred to the pair first. Ideal output:

$$
r_t'^{\mathrm{ideal}} = r_t + \mathrm{tokensIn}, \qquad
y'^{\mathrm{ideal}} = k / r_t'^{\mathrm{ideal}}, \qquad
\mathrm{gross} = y - y'^{\mathrm{ideal}}
$$

Then **cap**:

$$
\mathrm{ethOut} = \min(\mathrm{gross},\ 0.15 \cdot \texttt{reserveEth},\ \texttt{reserveEth})
$$

Paid from `reserveEth` as native ETH. Vault ETH is unreachable.

### Cap must re-derive $k$

When the 15% cap binds, `reserveToken` is re-derived from the ETH **actually** paid:

$$
r_t' = k / (y - \mathrm{ethOut})
$$

**Not** set to $r_t + \mathrm{tokensIn}$. Booking the full `tokensIn` against a capped `ethOut` permanently broke $k$ and let a cycle of sell-into-cap / buy-back drain real ETH. Extra tokens sit as unaccounted balance (same as a bare transfer). Oversizing a sell into the cap is wasteful for the sender, never profitable.

## Why vault ETH is not dumpable

Traders can only take `reserveEth`. Route fills, redemption peels and reallocation all move `vaultEth`, and none of them touch the AMM — a meme dump cannot reach the senior's collateral. The senior still has **gap risk** on it, because it is still ETH, which is what the [sell route](/protocol/routes) is for.

The mirror problem is that the AMM quotes off `juniorEth` but can only *pay* from `reserveEth`. On a rally part of the vault stops backing senior and starts belonging to the junior while sitting where a seller cannot reach it: see [reserve cover](/protocol/leverage#the-two-cover-ratios) and `rebalanceToReserve`.

Coin page copy labels the vault "not dumpable."
