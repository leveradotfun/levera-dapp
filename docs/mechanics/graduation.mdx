When the raise is met or the 800M is gone, `_graduate()`:

1. Marks the curve closed.
2. Wraps the raise as WETH and seeds `MemePair` (Uniswap V2-style $xy=k$).
3. If 2x is on, `_tryPair()` borrows idle LYC USDG, buys real ETH, and parks it in `vaultEth`.

## Two ETH buckets after that

| Bucket | What it is | Who can take it |
|---|---|---|
| `reserveEth` | AMM reserve | Anyone selling the meme (15% of reserve per swap) |
| `vaultEth` | Senior collateral | Route fills, redemption peels, netting, and `rebalanceToReserve` |

Fee ETH awaiting harvest is in neither. `poolEth = reserveEth + vaultEth`.

## Pairing (2x only)

A curve may graduate **and pair at 2x** iff leverage is on. Senior needed for $L^\star = 2$ is the raise in USD:

$$
S^\star = T \cdot P
$$

Idle USDG is what actually attaches:

$$
\mathrm{take} = \min(S^\star - S_{\mathrm{already}},\ U_{\mathrm{idle}})
$$

If the idle cannot cover $S^\star$, **do not fake 2x**. The coin lists at

$$
L = 1 + \frac{\mathrm{take}}{J}
$$

and `tryPair()` tops it up as deposits arrive. Occupancy rent and the 50 bps pairing fee are charged on the dollars actually attached.

### Worked full pair

6.9 ETH raise, ETH $= 3000$ USD:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{junior ETH} &= 6.9 \\
\text{senior ETH} &= 6.9 \quad \text{(bought with idle USDG)} \\
\text{pool} &= 13.8\ \mathrm{ETH} = 41400 \\
\text{LYC liability up} &= 20700 \\
J &= 20700 \\
L &= 2.0
\end{aligned}
$$

LYC shares are **not** minted at pair. The idle USDG already had shares outstanding; pairing converts cash to vault ETH and moves that slice of liability from idle to $S_i$. NAV unchanged by the conversion itself.

## Pairing fee

Once per dollar attached (partial bumps sum):

$$
\mathrm{pairingFee} = 50\ \mathrm{bps} \times \mathrm{take}
$$

Taken from **vault ETH**, swapped to USDG, `depositYield` (unminted). A coin that pairs, dumps, and never trades still pays for the attach and for the rebalancing that may follow. It is billed against the high-water senior, so senior that leaves and returns is not charged twice.

$20700$ USD paired $\times 50$ bps $= 103.50$ USD to LYC. Always.

Because the fee is paid from vault ETH, attaching senior slightly **raises** leverage. Accepted.

## Concentration

There is **no** hard per-launch cap. A hard cap made the first coin on an empty book unpairable, which is a launchpad that cannot launch. Scarcity is the occupancy curve: renting senior gets steeply more expensive as utilisation climbs, up to 70% APR against empty idle.

## 1x graduation

`leverageEnabled = false`: skip pairing entirely. AMM is vanilla $k = \texttt{reserveToken} \times \texttt{reserveEth}$. No occupancy, no vault, no LYC holder slice.
