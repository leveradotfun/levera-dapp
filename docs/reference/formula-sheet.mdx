## Leverage

$$
L = \frac{\mathrm{TVL}}{\mathrm{TVL} - S} = \frac{\mathrm{CR}}{\mathrm{CR}-1}, \qquad
\mathrm{CR} = \frac{\mathrm{TVL}}{S} = \frac{L}{L-1}
$$

$$
L^\star = 2, \qquad \mathrm{band} = [1.5,\ 2.5]
$$

$$
S^\star_{\mathrm{pair}} = T \cdot P
$$

ETH gap to junior wipe from an all-ETH 2x start: $50\%$. From $L$:

$$
\delta = 1 - \frac{1}{L}
$$

## The Earn Pool

$$
\mathcal{L} = \sum_i S_i + U_{\mathrm{idle}} + E_{\mathrm{idle}} \cdot P, \qquad
\mathrm{NAV} = \frac{\mathcal{L}}{\mathrm{supply}} \quad (\mathrm{NAV}:=1 \text{ if supply}=0)
$$

$$
\mathrm{CR}_{\mathrm{global}} = \frac{\sum_i \mathrm{TVL}_i + U_{\mathrm{idle}} + E_{\mathrm{idle}} \cdot P}{\mathcal{L}}
$$

$$
\mathrm{mint} = \frac{\mathrm{usd}}{\mathrm{NAV}}, \qquad
\mathrm{feeMint} = \frac{\mathrm{usdReceived}}{\mathrm{NAV}}
$$

Balance sheet identity:

$$
\mathcal{L} + \sum_i J_i = \sum_i \mathrm{TVL}_i + U_{\mathrm{idle}}
$$

## Bonding curve

$$
m = \frac{4}{3}, \qquad
V_{t0} = C m, \qquad
V_{u0} = (m-1)T, \qquad
k = V_{t0} V_{u0}
$$

$$
C = 8\times 10^8, \qquad T = 6.9\ \mathrm{ETH\ (default)}
$$

Buy: $\mathrm{fee} = 0.0125 \cdot \mathrm{ethIn}$, $V_u' = V_u + \mathrm{net}$, $V_t' = k/V_u'$, $\Delta t = V_t - V_t'$.

Listing: $P_{\mathrm{list}} = T / 2\times 10^8$.

## AMM

$$
y =
\begin{cases}
\texttt{reserveEth} & 1\mathrm{x} \\
J/P & 2\mathrm{x}
\end{cases}
\qquad
k = r_t \cdot y
\qquad
p = y / r_t
$$

Sell cap: $\mathrm{ethOut} \le 0.15 \cdot \texttt{reserveEth}$. If bound, $r_t' = k / (y - \mathrm{ethOut})$.

## Occupancy

$$
U = \frac{\mathcal{L} - U_{\mathrm{idle}}}{\mathcal{L}}, \qquad U^\star = 0.80
$$

$$
r(U) =
\begin{cases}
0.02 + 0.08 \cdot U/U^\star & U \le U^\star \\
0.10 + 0.60 \cdot (U-U^\star)/(1-U^\star) & U > U^\star
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\mathrm{owed} = \min\left( S_i \cdot r(U) \cdot \Delta t / Y,\ J_i \right), \quad 0 \text{ if } L_i \ge 2.5
$$

$Y = 365 \times 86400$.

## Piecewise-linear schedules

Every price and fee that reacts to the state of the book is one of these: control points with
linear interpolation between them and flat clamping outside.

$$
\text{segment}(x;\ x_0 \to x_1,\ y_0 \to y_1) =
\begin{cases}
y_0 & x \text{ at or before } x_0 \\
y_1 & x \text{ at or past } x_1 \\
y_0 + (y_1 - y_0)\dfrac{x - x_0}{x_1 - x_0} & \text{between}
\end{cases}
$$

$x_0 > x_1$ is allowed: half these curves are read as leverage rises and half as it falls.
Deterministic, bounded, and dependent only on the input and the control points — never on market
data, which is why a route's fill price is knowable before the transaction is sent.

## Rebalance routes

$$
\text{routePrice} = P \cdot f(L)
$$

$f$ is a three-point curve with the crossover at oracle spot:

| Route | Inner | Crossover | Outer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sell | $L = 2.20,\ f = 1.0050$ | $L = 2.50,\ f = 1$ | $L \ge 3.00,\ f = 0.9800$ |
| Buy | $L = 1.80,\ f = 0.9950$ | $L = 1.50,\ f = 1$ | $L \le 1.20,\ f = 1.0200$ |

Fill size to reach target, in closed form:

$$
d = \frac{\mathrm{TVL} - L^\star J}{P\left(L^\star f - (L^\star - 1)\right)}
\qquad\xrightarrow{\ f = 1\ }\qquad
d = \frac{\mathrm{TVL} - 2J}{P}
$$

Capped at $15\%$ of `vaultEth` per fill, and on the buy side at whatever idle cash exists.

Route P&L, booked signed per pool and landing on the junior:

$$
\Delta\texttt{routePnl} = \text{cashLeg} - d \cdot P
$$

## Reserve cover and the bucket correction

$$
\texttt{seniorCoverage} = \frac{\texttt{vaultEth}}{S/P}, \qquad
\texttt{reserveCover} = \frac{\texttt{reserveEth}}{\texttt{juniorEth}}
$$

$$
\texttt{juniorEth} = \texttt{reserveEth} + \texttt{vaultEth} - S/P
$$

`rebalanceToReserve` moves only the excess, leaving coverage at exactly $1$:

$$
\Delta E = \max\!\left(\texttt{vaultEth} - S/P,\ 0\right)
$$

TVL, $J$ and $L$ are unchanged — this changes which bucket the collateral sits in, nothing else.

## Pool-to-pool netting

$$
\Delta = \min\!\left(\texttt{seniorExcess}_{\text{src}},\ \texttt{seniorGap}_{\text{dst}}\right)
$$

$$
\texttt{seniorExcess} = \max(S - J,\ 0), \qquad \texttt{seniorGap} = \max(J - S,\ 0)
$$

Collateral and claim move together at $P$, so $\Delta J = 0$ on both sides and $\mathcal{L}$ is
unchanged — only its distribution across pools moves.

## Orphan sweep

The one remaining swap, confined to $J = 0$:

$$
\text{sell all of } \texttt{vaultEth} \to \text{USDG}, \quad S \mathrel{-}= \min(U_{\text{received}},\ S)
$$

Any residual above the claim has no owner and is deposited as yield.

## Fees (quote ETH)

$$
f(L) = 125\ \mathrm{bps} + \sigma(L)
$$

The flat 125 splits 2x: creator $30$, protocol $45$, holders $50$. 1x: creator $30$, protocol $95$,
holders $0$.

The surcharge $\sigma$ is not income — it goes into the AMM reserve as cushion:

$$
\sigma(L) =
\begin{cases}
\text{segment}(L;\ 2 \to 1.5,\ 0 \to 250\ \mathrm{bps}) & \text{buy (junior arrives)} \\
\text{segment}(L;\ 2 \to 2.5,\ 0 \to 250\ \mathrm{bps}) + \text{crSurcharge} & \text{sell (junior leaves)}
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\text{crSurcharge} = \text{segment}\!\left(\mathrm{CR}_{\mathrm{global}};\ 1.5 \to 1.0,\ 0 \to 400\ \mathrm{bps}\right)
$$

All-in is capped at the $700$ bps ceiling.

Pairing fee, billed against the high-water basis so the same dollars are never charged twice:

$$
\text{pairingFee} = 50\ \mathrm{bps} \times \max\!\left(S_{\text{after}} - \texttt{pairingBilled},\ 0\right)
$$

1x: creator $30$, protocol $95$, holders $0$.

Pairing: $50\ \mathrm{bps} \times \mathrm{take}$, unminted.

Redeem: $10$ bps unminted.

Swap: $\mathrm{minOut} = 0.99 \times$ oracle.

## Exit

Covered, and the feed is live: burn $s$, pay $s(1-0.001)\times\mathrm{NAV}$ as idle USDG first, then
WETH peeled in kind from the quietest 2x pools. No swap, so nothing can fail on liquidity. Remaining
CR does not fall.

$$
E_{\text{peeled}} = \frac{\text{shortfall}}{P}, \qquad S_i \mathrel{-}= E \cdot P \ \Rightarrow\ \Delta J_i = 0
$$

Impaired, **or the feed is dead**: pro-rata, and price-free — every term is a ratio of quantities.

$$
U_{\text{out}} = \frac{s}{\text{supply}} U_{\mathrm{idle}}, \qquad
E_{\text{out}} = \frac{s}{\text{supply}} \left(E_{\mathrm{idle}} + \sum_i \texttt{vaultEth}_i\right)
$$

Shares burned scale with what was actually delivered, so an unreachable remainder is not quietly
handed to whoever stayed.

## Earn Pool APY

On-chain, from a trailing window:

$$
\text{APY} = \frac{\text{yield}_{\text{window}}}{\tfrac{1}{2}\left(\mathcal{L}_0 + \mathcal{L}_1\right)} \cdot \frac{365\mathrm{d}}{\Delta t}
\qquad (\Delta t \ge 1\mathrm{h},\ \text{else } 0)
$$

$$
\text{yield} = \text{totalOccupancy} + \text{totalCashYield}
$$

The window rolls once a day and rolling early is a no-op, so it cannot be checkpointed at a
flattering moment. Liability is averaged because deposits arriving mid-window earned on a book that
grew.

This is **not** $r(U)$. See [the Earn Pool](/protocol/earn-pool).
