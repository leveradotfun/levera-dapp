## Slow dump

The sell route opens, fillers ratchet the senior into USDG, NAV holds, memes delever. This is the intended path — and it depends on somebody filling.

## Gap −50% one oracle print

Junior zero. LYC NAV $=$ leftover / supply, possibly below $\$1$. Pro-rata exit. This is **not** a bug. 2x means a *larger* gap than 3x, not no gap.

At 3x the same story happens at −33% ETH, with almost no time to `protect()`. That is why 3x was rejected.

## Thin USDG pool

`protect()` and fee swaps revert on `minOut` (99% of oracle). Trading on that launch can halt until depth returns. Better than a bad fill socialized onto LYC. Do not widen `minOut` to "make it work."

## Empty idle

No 2x pairs at full size. Coins can still sell on the curve; they graduate into whatever idle exists (`take = min(gap, idle)`). Occupancy rent and the 50 bps pairing fee are charged on the dollars actually attached, not on a ghost 2x. Never mint ghost senior.

## 2-minute coin, huge volume

LYC NAV jumps from the 50 bps holder slice (after harvest) + pairing bps. Occupancy $\approx 0$. Correct.

## 2-minute coin, zero volume, instant dump

Pairing bps is the only yield. `protect()` may slip against junior. LYC should still be whole if the move is inside −50% and txs land. If not, the gap case.

## Protocol sells fee-LYC

Equivalent to having been paid USDG. Depositors were not diluted at mint (assets in, then shares out). Fee-minted shares are liquid; alignment is that the treasury holds the senior of live 2x pools, not a time-lock.

## All launches are ETH

Isolation does not save LYC from one crash. USDG idle is the only diversifier until another collateral type exists.

## Junior mint at NAV without new senior

$L$ falls. Below $1.8$ the buy route opens for anyone who wants to fill it. Do not steal the minter's collateral into senior.

## Keeper lag

`protect()` is 15% of vault per tx. $L$ can print above $2.5$ between ticks. Occupancy is a timestamp index (still owed). Unharvested fees sit uncounted until someone calls `harvest()`. Anyone can call the same functions.

## Pause

Mint / pair / protect / harvest halt. Curve/AMM buys and LYC redeem stay up so people can exit. Peels that need `protect()` fail while paused if idle is short. See [Pause](/security/pause).

## 15% caps are griefable

Slow unwind, not a one-shot drain. Accepted.

## Wash volume

Same-wallet reverse wipes `recentVolumeUsd`. Two-wallet wash can still game reallocation ranking. Residual accepted; treat ranking as adversarial.

## Fresh but wrong oracle

Stale/wide feed fail-closes. A *fresh but wrong* price moves leverage, protect size, `minOut`, and funding together. No second feed yet.
