## Senior / junior

HFyc is a dollar-senior that **starts** at $1$ and **rises** only when assets enter without a matching mint (fees, pairing, funding). The memecoin is the residual on ETH. When ETH dumps, the protocol **sells ETH and takes the senior with the cash**, starting at $2.5\times$, restoring $2\times$. When the residual is gone, $1$ is over and everyone remaining is pro-rata.

No loop. No second token. No naked mint. No APR that pretends a 2-minute coin paid rent.

## Isolation

Each launch is an isolated vault (an EIP-1167 clone). HFyc accounting is global.

- A bug in one launch cannot `transferFrom` another launch's ETH.
- An ETH crash hits every ETH pool together. Isolation does **not** diversify beta.
- USDG idle is the only diversifier until another collateral type exists.

On Robinhood Chain the cash leg is **USDG**, marked at the USDG/USD feed. Pretending cash is $1$ is how a "dollar senior" quietly becomes a junior on the stable.

## Issue price is not a peg

HFyc is **not** a stablecoin. There is no mechanism that prints dollars, borrows dollars, or socializes a shortfall onto a backstop to hold $\mathrm{NAV} = 1$.

- At genesis, $\mathrm{supply} = 0$ and $\mathrm{NAV} := 1$ by definition.
- Deposits mint at the **current** NAV. If NAV is $1.07$, $107$ of newly deposited USDG mints $100$ HFyc. Existing holders are not diluted.
- If junior is wiped system-wide and $\mathrm{CR} \le 1$, redeem is pro-rata of leftover assets. The print can be below $1$. Named, not a bug. See [Failure modes](/protocol/failure-modes).

## Who gets ETH upside

Start from a just-paired 2x book, funding off, ETH $= 3000$ USD, raise $= 6.9$ ETH:

$$
\begin{aligned}
E &= 13.8\ \mathrm{ETH} = 41400 \\
S &= 20700 \\
J &= 20700 \\
L &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

ETH $+10\%$:

$$
\mathrm{TVL} = 45540,\quad S = 20700,\quad J = 24840 \quad (+20\% = 2 \times 10\%)
$$

HFyc is still $20700$. NAV unchanged.

ETH $-10\%$:

$$
\mathrm{TVL} = 37260,\quad S = 20700,\quad J = 16560 \quad (-20\%),\quad L = 2.25
$$

Still inside the band. HFyc does not get the moon. HFyc does not take the first 50% of a dump. That is the whole split.

## Copy on the coin page

2x is **rented**. If `protect()` fires, the coin's leverage falls toward $2.0$ on a smaller ETH book, or toward $1\times$ if senior leaves. NAV of remaining junior is not robbed by a fair unwind; **future** ETH beta is smaller.

> 2x ETH, variable. If ETH dumps, the vault sells to USDG to protect HFyc and this coin's leverage falls. HFyc can exit. This ticker can go to zero at a 50% ETH gap if that gap is one print.
