Occupancy is **loop carry**: what a 2x coin pays the Earn Pool for attached senior. It is a timestamp index, not a 1 Hz keeper. A 2-minute coin pays nothing material. That is correct.

<Warning>
  Do **not** put `fundingRateWad` on a page as "APY". Holder return is NAV since the $\$1$ issue price. Occupancy is off at $L \ge 2.5$. Charging rent while selling the cushion accelerates the wipe.
</Warning>

## Accrual on touch

On any touch of pool $i$ (trade, protect, redeem, pair, harvest, or permissionless `accrueFunding` / `EarnPool.accruePools`):

$$
\Delta t = t_{\mathrm{now}} - t_{\mathrm{last},i}
$$

$$
\mathrm{owed} =
\begin{cases}
0 & L_i \ge 2.5 \ \mathrm{or}\ J_i = 0 \ \mathrm{or}\ \text{not paired} \\
S_i \cdot r(U) \cdot \Delta t / Y & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\mathrm{owed} \leftarrow \min(\mathrm{owed},\ J_i)
$$

then $S_i \mathrel{+}= \mathrm{owed}$, $t_{\mathrm{last},i} \leftarrow t_{\mathrm{now}}$. TVL is unchanged, junior shrinks, Earn Pool liability rises with **no mint**. NAV up.

$Y = 365 \times 86400$ seconds. `owed` is capped at junior so rent can consume the junior, never push it negative.

## Kinked utilisation curve

The live book prices **scarcity** rather than charging a flat rate. A hard 10% per-launch cap made the first coin on an empty book unpairable; the rate does that job instead.

Let $U$ be utilisation of the senior book:

$$
U = \frac{\mathcal{L} - U_{\mathrm{idle}}}{\mathcal{L}} \in [0,1]
$$

(deployed senior over the whole liability; idle cash is the only "available", because deposits are converted on arrival rather than queued.)

Kink at $U^\star = 0.80$:

$$
r(U) =
\begin{cases}
r_0 + r_1 \cdot (U / U^\star) & U \le U^\star \\
r_0 + r_1 + r_2 \cdot (U - U^\star)/(1 - U^\star) & U > U^\star
\end{cases}
$$

with

$$
r_0 = 2\%,\quad r_1 = 8\%,\quad r_2 = 60\%
$$

| $U$ | $r(U)$ |
|---|---|
| $0$ | $2\%$ APR |
| $80\%$ | $10\%$ APR |
| $100\%$ | $70\%$ APR |

Between touches the book may have gone from flush to starved; accrual reads the **live** $r(U)$ at the moment of the touch.

## Pending is not NAV

Views and the research files may show **pending** occupancy so a UI does not wait on a bot. Pending is **informational**. Redeem calls `_settleOccupancy` first (up to 32 pools per tx, rotating via `occupancyCursor`) so a bag cannot exit against a stale index.

Do not fold pending into redeemable NAV in the client. `EarnPool.nav()` is settled liability / supply.

`EarnPool.accruePools(which)` walks up to 32 registered pools and `try/catch`es so one revert cannot block the rest.

## Honest quoting

| Number | What it is | How to show it |
|---|---|---|
| `fundingRateWad` $= r(U)$ | What 2x coins currently pay on attached senior | Label it "occupancy rate". **Never** "APY". |
| `earnPoolApyWad` | Realised holder return over the trailing window, annualised | This is the APY. See [the Earn Pool](/protocol/earn-pool). |
| `earnPoolYieldMixWad` | The split between rent and cash income | Publish it beside the APY, always. |

The two are not close, and conflating them is the single most misleading thing this product could
print. $r(U)$ describes a stream; `earnPoolApyWad` describes what holders actually earned, and in a
fee-driven book that is dominated by trade and pairing income rather than rent.

A continuous-compounding conversion $e^{r}-1$ of the $70\%$ fully-deployed rate ($\approx 101\%$) is
**not** a holder yield and must never be shown as one. `earnPoolYieldMixWad` exists so nobody has to
infer the difference from two lifetime counters.
