Levera is a memecoin launchpad. Coins are the product. **LYC** (Levera Yield Coin) is dollar-senior credit that **2x** coins rent, held in the **Earn Pool**. There is no Morpho, no mint/burn of the meme after launch, and no donating the raise into a Uniswap LP so dumpers can steal it.

<Info>
  $1 is the **issue price** of LYC and the healthy-NAV target. It is not a peg the protocol defends with someone else's collateral.
</Info>

## One-sentence product

People deposit USDG (or ETH sold to USDG on arrival) and hold **LYC** — a single USD-senior token that starts at $\$1$ and only rises from real inflows. Memecoins are the junior claim on the same ETH: they take **all** collateral price movement, pay LYC in fees and occupancy rent, and can be delevered when ETH dumps.

## The split

| Claim | Token | What they get | What they pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Senior | LYC | Dollar NAV. No ETH upside once paired. | First-loss **after** junior is gone (−50% ETH gap at 2x). |
| Junior | The memecoin | 100% of ETH delta, levered toward $2\times$. | Trade fees + occupancy rent. Leverage is rented and can fall. |
| Protocol | Fee-minted LYC | Always liquid. Aligned with the senior. | Never paid raw ETH into the treasury. |
| Creator | ETH, or LYC if 2x and opted in | 30 bps of the 125 bps trade fee. | Toggle is immutable at create. 1x creators are ETH-only. |

## Two kinds of coin

Toggle **2x** at creation. The flag is immutable.

- **1x.** Ordinary bonding curve, then a TOKEN/ETH AMM. Never pulls LYC. Trade fee is 30 bps creator / 95 bps protocol / **0 to LYC holders**. Creator fees are ETH only.
- **2x.** Same curve. At graduation the coin borrows idle Earn Pool cash, buys ETH, and parks it in `vaultEth`. Spot is priced off **junior ETH** so it moves $\sim 2\times$ with ETH **without** putting vault ETH in the dumpable book. Trade fee is 30 / 45 / 50 plus a leverage surcharge that stays in the pool as cushion. Occupancy rent and the pairing 50 bps apply.

<Warning>
  2x means a **−50% ETH gap in one oracle print** wipes junior. Further drop is first-loss on LYC. That is the product, not a bug.
</Warning>

## The three numbers that run the book

For every launch pool $i$:

$$
\mathrm{TVL}_i = E_i \cdot P + U_i, \qquad
J_i = \max(\mathrm{TVL}_i - S_i, 0), \qquad
L_i = \frac{\mathrm{TVL}_i}{J_i}
$$

where $E_i$ is pool ETH, $P$ is the ETH/USD oracle, $U_i$ is USDG in the pool (normally zero — cash leaves to idle), $S_i$ is attached senior, and $J_i$ is junior (meme) NAV.

Target leverage is $L^\star = 2$. As a pool drifts away from it the protocol **posts a route** — a
price at which anyone can take collateral off it, or give collateral to it — and where two pools want
opposite things they are [netted directly](/protocol/netting) with no market involved at all. The
protocol never trades for itself, so the moment it most needs to de-risk is not the moment a thin
book can stop it. See [Leverage and vLYC](/protocol/leverage) and [Rebalance routes](/protocol/routes).

The Earn Pool itself:

$$
\mathcal{L} = \sum_i S_i + U_{\mathrm{idle}} + E_{\mathrm{idle}} \cdot P, \qquad
\mathrm{NAV} = \frac{\mathcal{L}}{\mathrm{supply}} \quad (\text{\$1 if supply}=0)
$$

NAV rises **only** when value arrives without new deposit shares: occupancy rent, the pairing 50 bps,
the harvested 50 bps holder slice of 2x trades, and the spread the protocol earns for de-risking near
the inner bound of a route. APY is [measured over a trailing window](/protocol/earn-pool), never
promised — and it is not the occupancy rate.
