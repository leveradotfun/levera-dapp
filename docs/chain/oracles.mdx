Every USD figure in the system — NAV, leverage, rebalance sizing, `minOut`, cover — is computed off the oracle, never off a pool spot. Reading Uniswap for NAV or for a rebalance price is self-referential manipulation.

## Bounds

`OracleLib`:

$$
\mathrm{conf} \le 0.02 \cdot P, \qquad t_{\mathrm{now}} - t_{\mathrm{published}} \le 25\mathrm{h}
$$

The Robinhood ETH/USD heartbeat is **24 h**. A 1-hour staleness window would fail closed on a quiet day even though the feed is still the live one, so the band matches the feed. A tighter action-path window belongs on a chain with a 1 h heartbeat.

Reverts:

- `oracle: no price` if $P = 0$
- `oracle: stale` if older than 25 h
- `oracle: confidence too wide` if conf $> 2\%$ of mid

Same checks on the cash feed (`readCash`).

## Two prices

| Feed | Used for |
|---|---|
| ETH/USD | $\mathrm{TVL}_i$, $J_i$, $L_i$, occupancy USD, swap `minOut` on ETH legs, raise USD snapshot |
| USDG/USD | Idle cash in `totalAssetsUsd` / `globalCr`. A depeg must show up in cover. |

Cash is **not** assumed $\$1$.

## Swap floor

$$
\mathrm{minOut} = 0.99 \times \text{oracle conversion}
$$

`SWAP_TOLERANCE_WAD = 0.99e18`. Applies to the orphan sweep, fee swaps, idle conversions, `mintWithEth` and `acquireSenior`. The rebalance routes have no `minOut` at all — they post a price rather than taking one. Revert rather than fill a skewed venue.

On a real AMM the same 1% is a sandwich ceiling — add deadlines / private relay before mainnet money.

## What fail-closed does *not* catch

A **fresh but wrong** price moves leverage, protect size, `minOut`, and funding together. No second feed yet. Trusted-role table in [Threat model](/security/threat-model).
